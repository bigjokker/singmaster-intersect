# No extra left-half column for \(C(F_{20}F_{21},F_{18}F_{21})\)

This note records a theorem about one binomial value. It does **not**
prove Singmaster’s conjecture. The image lemma, the two-band split, and
the size law are the same as in [`i8-N6.md`](i8-N6.md); this note only
records the i=9 exhaustion and the proof object.

## 1. Statement

Let \(F_n\) be the Fibonacci sequence with \(F_1=1\), \(F_2=1\). Write

\[
N=F_{20}F_{21}=74049690,\qquad
K=F_{18}F_{21}=28284464,\qquad
m=C(N,K).
\]

(The complementary family column is \(K+1=F_{19}F_{20}=28284465\).)
Then

\[
\log_{10}m=21386569.341068,\qquad
k_{\max}=35522329,
\]

where \(k_{\max}\) is the largest integer with \(C(2k,k)\le m\). There
is no central solution \(C(2k,k)=m\). The Lind–Singmaster–Tovey identity
supplies exactly two off-centre left-half pairs, so \(N(m)\ge 6\).

**Theorem.** There is no pair \((n,k)\) with \(2\le k\le n/2\),
\(k\notin\{K,K+1\}\), and \(C(n,k)=m\). Therefore \(N(m)=6\).

Extra left-half columns can only occur for

\[
2\le k\le k_{\max},\qquad k\notin\{K,K+1\},
\]

which is \(35{,}522{,}326\) columns.

## 2. Exhaustion

Every one of those columns has a recorded prime \(p\) with
\(r(p):=m\bmod p\notin I_{p,k}\). The certificates live in
`results/i9_witness.npz` (sha256 of the \((k,p)\) tables
`e375de140d32cf77e9b61bbdb67cf77a7a2308367ba5005a8586bfcbbb3f80be`).
A column is absent from \(\{K,K+1\}\) by construction.

| range | method | columns | outcome |
|---|---|---:|---|
| \(2..80\) | modular, then `witness fill` | \(79\) | all impossible |
| \(81..K-1\) | family_sweep Z-jump, cap \(12\) live primes; four leftovers killed by full Lucas on \(p\le\sqrt{N}\) | \(28{,}284{,}383\) | all killed |
| \(K+2..k_{\max}\) | family_sweep Band II, \(p>N/2\) | \(7{,}237{,}864\) | all killed, death at pass \(9\) |

Band II died on the pre-registered size-law curve (pass 1 left \(703{,}000\)
against a \(702{,}981\) prediction; even-\(g\) rose \(0.66\to 1\); mean
surviving \(k\) walked down toward \(K\)).

Z-jump `json.clean=false` is the correct sweep flag: two-digit \(r(p)\)
is false for \(p\le\sqrt{N}=8605\), so the sweep never scanned those
primes. Cap-12 left \(k=87,399,553,1281\). Full Lucas on the first live
prime below \(\sqrt{N}\) killed all four. They are in the npz, as are
\(k=2..80\).

`scripts/coverage_ledger.py` checks
\(\mathrm{witnessed}(9)=[2,k_{\max}]\setminus\{K,K+1\}\) exactly, with
\(k_{\max}\) recomputed from \(N,K\). A random sample of \(5{,}000\)
certificates independently re-checks as kills
(`scripts/witness.py verify --sample 5000`): \(0\) invalid.

JSON: `results/i9_sweep.json`. The jsonl is the run log, gitignored once
the npz exists; keep it on disk until a full (not sampled) verify is
done, if that is ever wanted.

## 3. What this is not

- Not Singmaster’s conjecture (a bound on \(N(t)\) for every \(t\)).
- Not a family theorem. i=2..8 are closed the same way; i=10 is a
  different cost, not a corollary.
- Not a licence to build \(m_{9}\) or \(m_{10}\), or to treat
  `json.clean=false` as a new \(N=8\).

The 2022 interior theorem of Matomäki–Radziwiłł–Shao–Tao–Teräväinen does
not apply at this \(N\): the effective threshold from the proof is far
larger than \(\ln N_9=18.1\). The census is the theorem.
